# Email Drafting Skill

## Methodology
1. **Define Objective & Audience**: Specify target persona, stage in relationship, and singular call to action.
2. **Subject Lines**: Generate 3 high-open subject line variants (< 45 characters, clear value hook).
3. **Persuasion Architecture**: Hook -> Empathy & Pain Point -> Unique Value Proposition -> Low-Friction CTA.
4. **Tone & Brevity**: Keep body under 150 words for cold outreach; maintain conversational, professional voice.

## Output Format
- Subject Line Options (A/B testing variants)
- Email Body Draft
- Follow-up Sequence Outline (Day 3, Day 7)
