# Market Research Skill

## Methodology
1. **Identify Market Scope**: Define the industry vertical, target geography, customer segments, and addressable market (TAM, SAM, SOM).
2. **Web Discovery**: Conduct targeted web searches across reputable trade journals, industry analysts (Gartner, IDC, Statista), and primary sources.
3. **Verify Trends & Drivers**: Extract quantifiable tailwinds, headwinds, regulatory impacts, and customer adoption metrics.
4. **Cite Sources**: Provide source URLs and publication dates for all statistical claims.

## Output Format
- Executive Overview
- Market Size & Growth Projections
- Key Industry Drivers & Catalysts
- Potential Risks & Headwinds
- Verified Sources & Citations
